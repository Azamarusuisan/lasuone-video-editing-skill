# Assets and rights

The source snapshot inventories 189 public channel items and all 189 have `RIGHTS_STATUS: UNKNOWN`; they are research references, not production assets. Their rights need not be acquired merely to consult the recorded observations. They may not be copied into a production unless selected and separately cleared. No generated assets are currently packaged. Research availability is not reuse permission.

Procure in this order: user-provided → user-owned → permitted channel archive → official distribution → licensed → Creative Commons with compatible terms → generated. Record per asset: stable ID/path, `SOURCE`, `RIGHTS_STATUS`, `USAGE_REASON`, owner/licensor, permission evidence, production use, redistribution/bundling permission, restrictions, and integrity value.

For a generated insert also record `GENERATED_ASSET: true`, generator/model, prompt, reference-image source and permission, generation date, intended meaning, destination timecode, placement reason, and a disclosure/track label. A reference image may guide Sol only when the user supplied it or its use is authorized. Do not clone protected characters or imply a generated depiction is real footage, evidence, an actual result, or a real news image.

For selected production assets, acceptable statuses are `USER_OWNED`, `LICENSED`, `OFFICIAL_PRESS` when its terms cover the use, `CREATIVE_COMMONS` when its terms cover the use, `PERMITTED_CHANNEL_ARCHIVE`, and `GENERATED` with valid source/reference rights. A selected `UNKNOWN` asset fails; an unselected research reference does not. Validate selected fonts, BGM, SE, logos, maps, articles, social captures, footage, and generated references independently. Require redistribution permission only when the asset will actually be bundled or distributed.
